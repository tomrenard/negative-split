Negative Split

90 km bike leg of a 70.3. You set the effort, the race runs at 20x and slows down when something happens. A run estimate follows.

Controls
W S
Effort gear up / down (swipe up / down)
A D
Passing lane / keep right (swipe left / right)

1 2
Grab a gel / bottle at an aid station

Rules

12 m draft zone behind every rider. More than 25 s inside one while the referee is close: 5 min penalty. Above threshold you burn matches. Eat too little and you bonk; eat faster than your gut absorbs and you slow down.

Finish

Power, matches and true fuel over the bike
